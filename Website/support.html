Support

Skyline Architect runs on a Mac, an iPad and an iPhone.

Getting started

Choose Tutorial in the main menu: it walks you through your first tower step by step. The full manual is also in the game, under the book button or Help ▸ Skyline Architect Manual on a Mac.

Common questions

Nobody moves in. Prospects need a lobby, a way up (stairs or an elevator), and services: electricity, water, climate and data from plant rooms. The leasing panel shows why prospects declined.

Where are my saves? On your device only, in the game's own folder. The Load Game screen lists them.

The text is too small. Choose Text Size in the main menu (Standard, Large, Larger or Largest).

Contact

Found a bug or have a question? E-mail support@skyline-architect.com. Please mention your device, the game's version (on the App Store page, or on a Mac under Skyline Architect ▸ About) and what you did just before the problem.
