Privacy policy

Last updated: 29 September 2026

Skyline Architect collects no personal data.

  • The game has no account, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking, and it contains no third-party code that does.
  • It never connects to the internet. Nothing you do in the game is sent to us or to anyone else.
  • Your saves, settings, mods and scenario records are stored only on your device, in the game's own folder. Deleting the game deletes them.
  • The game asks for no permissions: no location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone.

If a future version offers to sync your saves through iCloud, they will go to your own iCloud account, under Apple's privacy terms, and only when you turn it on. This policy will be updated before that happens.

Questions: see Support.
