# Fire and incidents

## Fire

A fire can start in any room; worn rooms and plant rooms are more at risk. It grows every minute, damages the room and can spread to the rooms beside, above and below it.

- The **Fire Control Room** runs sprinklers for 15 floors up and down. Sprinklers slow a fire down and make it far less likely to start or spread. Without them a fire can burn for three quarters of an hour and take out several rooms; with them it is usually out within minutes.
- The **fire brigade** arrives about 16 minutes after the fire starts and helps put it out.
- Everyone **evacuates by the stairs**: nobody uses the elevators, and nobody goes in until the fire is out. Afterwards people return to work or home.

When the fire is out, the repairs are charged, repair jobs open for your technicians, tenants in badly damaged units leave, and your reputation drops.

## Weather incidents

| Incident | When | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| **Storm damage** | storms | a room is damaged and dirtied; $4,000 |
| **Power outage** | storms and heatwaves | the electrical plant fails: no power or light until a technician repairs it |
| **Burst pipe** | snow and frost | the water plant is damaged; $2,500 |

## Following an incident

A banner above the status pill tells you what is happening. **Show** moves the camera to the fire. The **Incidents** panel (`⌥⌘I`) lists fires in progress, with when the brigade arrives, and the last twelve incidents.

Keep technicians on staff: every incident leaves repair work behind (see [Utilities and staff](facilities.md)).
