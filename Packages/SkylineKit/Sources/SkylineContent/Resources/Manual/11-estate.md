# Cities and land

Your company can own buildings in more than one city. The whole estate runs on one clock and shares one bank account.

## Cities

| City | Character | Rents | Building costs | Demand | Taxes | Energy |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Port Calder** | Harbour city on reclaimed land; deep soft ground | 100 % | 100 % | 100 % | 100 % | 100 % |
| **Harrowgate** | The inland capital: old money, high rents, dear builders, granite close under the streets | 135 % | 125 % | 115 % | 120 % | 115 % |
| **Saltmere** | A quiet harbour town on marshland: cheap land and labour, few tenants | 75 % | 80 % | 85 % | 85 % | 90 % |

Each city has its own tenant market and its own weather.

## Buying land

Open **Estate** (`⌥⌘K`). Under **Land for sale** each plot shows its frontage, how deep you may dig and its price:

| Plot | City | Frontage | Price |
|---|---|---|---|
| Ferry Lane Corner | Port Calder | 32 m | $900,000 |
| Crown Yard | Harrowgate | 44 m | $2,400,000 |
| Harbour Row | Saltmere | 56 m | $450,000 |

**Buy** pays for the plot, with a foundation ready to build on. A purchase cannot be undone.

## Switching buildings

The Estate panel lists your properties with their floors, tenants, people, class and the last 24 hours' result. **Go** takes you there. The undo history starts afresh in each building.
