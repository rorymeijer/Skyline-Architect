# Amenities and visitors

Shops, restaurants, a fitness club, a cinema, a theatre and a sky bar serve the people who live and work in your tower, and draw visitors from the street. Each is run by an **operator** who rents the room like any other tenant. Without an operator the room stays closed.

| Amenity | Open | Seats per m | Visitors spend | Your share | Unlocks |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Shop | 09:00–21:00 | 0.8 | $30 | 8 % | the start |

## Who comes

- **Your own people**: office workers go out for lunch, residents in the evening, when there is a seat for them.
- **Street visitors**: every hour a number of people walk in, more in each amenity's busy hours, with good demand and weather and a good reputation. The sky bar draws more people the higher it is.

Visitors use your lobby and elevators, stay for a while and never stay past closing time. During a fire they leave.

## What you earn

You earn twice: the operator pays rent like any tenant, and at the 06:00 closing you receive your **share of the takings** (booked as "Turnover"). Select an amenity to see its customers and takings for today and yesterday.

Every kind of amenity that is open in a building also makes its other units more attractive to tenants.
