# Scenarios

A scenario gives you a start, a time limit and objectives. Open **Scenarios…** from the main menu or the File menu, pick one to read its briefing, objectives and rules, and press **Start**.

## Winning and losing

- Your objectives are measured at every **daily closing** (06:00), over your whole estate.
- You win when all of them are met at once, at as many closings in a row as the scenario asks for.
- You lose when you go bankrupt, or when the last day's closing passes without a win.

The **Objectives** panel (`⌥⌘O`) shows each objective with its progress, how many closings are left and how many in a row you have held, the scenario's rules and its news.

After the result you can **Keep Playing** on the same estate as free play, go back to the scenarios, or return to the main menu.

## Objectives

| Objective | Counts |
|---|---|
| Population | residents and workers (not staff) |
| Units let | units with a tenant or owner |
| Cash | cash after the closing |
| Daily profit | the day's operating result: rent and running costs, not construction |
| Building class | the best class reached |
| Reputation | the best reputation |
| Average elevator wait | a maximum; banks with enough riders |
| Properties | plots you own |

## Stars and points

A won scenario earns up to **three stars**: one for winning, one for winning in the first 60 % of the time, and one when every objective beats its target by 25 %. Points reward winning early, beating the targets and a good reputation. Your best result per scenario is kept on this device (and in iCloud if you use it).

## Events and rules

Scenarios can script **events**: news, a rush or a slump in demand, a grant for reaching a milestone, a fine for missing one, a change in the weather, or a fire. Some also set **rules**: rooms you may not build (their tools show a lock), a height limit, a loan limit, fixed rents, or no staff.

## The scenarios

| Scenario | Difficulty | Goal |
|---|---|---|
| **First Tower (Tutorial)** | Easy | Your first building, step by step; let four units |
| **Opening Day** | Easy | 16 units let and a daily profit of $12,000 in 10 days |
| **Harbour Revival** | Medium | 100 people and a profit in Saltmere, held for 3 days |
| **Three Properties** | Medium | Three properties, 160 people and a daily profit of $10,000 |
| **Crown Prestige** | Hard | A Class A building in Harrowgate with short elevator waits |
| **Skyline** | Hard | 600 people with waits under a minute, in profit, for a week |
| **Lean Tower** | Hard | An office tower of twelve floors at fixed rents, with no staff |

## The tutorial

*First Tower* shows a **tutorial panel** on the left: the step to do now, with a link to the chapter of this manual that explains it, and the list of steps with a tick for each one done. The game starts paused, so you can build the first rooms in peace. Close the panel with its × and bring it back with the graduation-cap button in the view controls.
